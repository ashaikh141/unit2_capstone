MERIDIAN SYSTEMS - 2025 EMPLOYEE ENGAGEMENT SURVEY REPORT
Prepared by People Operations. Survey period: September 2025. Response rate: 87% (412 of 474 employees).

1. HEADLINE RESULTS
- Overall engagement score: 71% favorable (2024: 74%).
- Employee Net Promoter Score (eNPS): +18 (2024: +24).
- Voluntary annual turnover: 17% (2024: 14%).
- Intent to stay 2+ years: 63%.

2. SCORES BY DIMENSION (percent favorable)
- Relationship with direct manager: 82%
- Flexibility and remote work: 80%
- Team collaboration: 77%
- Company mission and purpose: 74%
- Compensation and benefits: 64%
- Workload and work-life balance: 61%
- Career growth and promotion opportunities: 58%
- Recognition: 60%

3. KEY FINDINGS
Strengths: Employees value their managers and the hybrid work model. Open comments praise supportive team leads and schedule flexibility.

Concerns: Career growth is the lowest-scoring area. Employees report unclear promotion criteria and long waits between promotion cycles. Workload scores dropped 8 points year over year, particularly in Engineering and Customer Support, where on-call rotations and EMEA coverage gaps lead to extra hours. Compensation satisfaction is lower among employees with 3-5 years of tenure, who feel salary bands have not kept pace with market rates.

4. DEPARTMENT VIEW
- Engineering: 68% engagement, highest turnover (21%).
- Customer Support: 63% engagement, driven by workload and shift coverage.
- Sales: 72% engagement, with concerns about quota changes mid-year.
- Customer Success: 74% engagement.
- G&A: 79% engagement.

5. RECOMMENDED ACTIONS
People Operations recommends publishing clear promotion criteria, shortening the promotion cycle, reviewing compensation bands, adding support coverage in EMEA, and limiting on-call rotation frequency.
