MERIDIAN SYSTEMS - CUSTOMER COMPLAINT HANDLING PROCEDURE
Owner: Head of Customer Support. Version 2.4.

1. PURPOSE
Every complaint is an opportunity to retain a customer and improve the product. This procedure ensures complaints are handled quickly, fairly, and consistently.

2. HOW COMPLAINTS ARRIVE
Complaints may arrive via support portal, email, phone, account managers, or social media. Whoever receives a complaint must log it in the support system the same day.

3. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
Customers receive an acknowledgement within 4 business hours containing a case number, the assigned owner, and the expected next update time.

4. SEVERITY TIERS AND RESOLUTION TARGETS
- Critical (service outage, data issue, major billing error): resolution target 24 hours, immediate escalation to a support manager.
- High (key feature failing, repeated unresolved issue): 3 business days.
- Standard (usability, minor billing, general dissatisfaction): 7 business days.

5. ESCALATION
Unresolved High complaints escalate to the Support Manager after 2 days. Complaints from accounts above $100,000 annual contract value are copied to the Customer Success Director and the account executive. Any complaint mentioning cancellation or legal action goes to the VP of Customer Success immediately.

6. RESOLUTION AUTHORITY
Support agents may issue credits up to $250. Support managers may approve up to $2,500. Larger amounts require VP approval.

7. ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS
Critical and repeat complaints require a written root cause analysis within 5 business days, shared with the customer when appropriate. Findings feed into the monthly product and support review.

8. FOLLOW-UP
Seven days after closure, the customer receives a satisfaction survey. Low scores (below 3 out of 5) trigger a personal call from a manager.

9. COMPLAINT TRENDS (Q4 2025)
Customer satisfaction on resolved cases averaged 4.3 out of 5. The top complaint categories were onboarding delays (31%), billing errors (22%), and slow support response in the EMEA region (18%). The EMEA response issue is being addressed by adding a second support shift.
