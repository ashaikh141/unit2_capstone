HALDEN RESEARCH GROUP - 2025 SAAS WORKFORCE AND CUSTOMER BENCHMARKS
Sample: 340 mid-market B2B software companies (200-1,000 employees).

1. EMPLOYEE ENGAGEMENT BENCHMARKS
- Median overall engagement: 76% favorable. Top quartile: 82%.
- Median eNPS: +28. Top quartile: +40.
- Median voluntary turnover: 13%. Software engineering roles: 15%.
- Median favorable score for career growth: 66%.
- Median favorable score for compensation: 68%.
- Median favorable score for workload and work-life balance: 70%.
- Median favorable score for manager relationship: 78%.
- Median favorable score for flexibility: 74%.

2. WHAT DRIVES ENGAGEMENT IN TOP PERFORMERS
Companies in the top quartile typically publish transparent promotion criteria, run promotion cycles at least twice a year, benchmark compensation every 12 months, cap on-call rotations, and offer individual learning budgets of $1,000 or more. Companies with annual-only promotion cycles show an average of 6 points lower career growth scores.

3. CUSTOMER BENCHMARKS
- Median annual customer churn (revenue-based, B2B SaaS mid-market): 8%. Top quartile: 5%.
- Median net revenue retention: 108%. Top quartile: 118%.
- Median customer satisfaction on support cases: 4.4 out of 5.
- Median time to first value after onboarding: 30 days.

4. SALES AND GROWTH BENCHMARKS
- Median annual revenue growth: 16%.
- Median sales cycle for mid-market deals: 74 days.
- Companies that tie part of sales compensation to retention or renewal show 2 points lower churn on average.
- Companies with structured onboarding (milestone-based) report 30% fewer early-life churn events.

5. NOTE ON USE
Benchmarks are medians and vary by segment. Comparisons should account for company size and customer mix.
