MERIDIAN SYSTEMS - EMPLOYEE POLICY HANDBOOK (Excerpt)
Owner: People Operations. Effective: March 2025.

1. WORK ARRANGEMENTS
Meridian operates a hybrid model. Employees are expected in the office 2 days per week (Tuesday and Thursday) and may work remotely on other days. Fully remote arrangements require VP approval and are reviewed annually. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time.

2. PAID TIME OFF
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per year, plus 10 company holidays and 5 sick days. Up to 5 unused days carry over. Employees with 5 or more years of tenure receive 25 days.

3. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Performance is reviewed once per year in November, with an informal check-in each quarter. Ratings use a four-point scale. Ratings inform salary adjustments and promotion eligibility.

4. PROMOTIONS
Promotions are considered once per year, effective January 1. Managers nominate employees in December. Promotion criteria are defined by job level in internal career frameworks, which are available on request from People Operations. Employees must spend at least 18 months in a level before being considered.

5. COMPENSATION
Salary bands are reviewed every 18 months against market data. Merit increases are awarded annually and average 3% to 4%. Employees below the midpoint of their band may receive additional adjustments at the manager's request.

6. LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT
Each employee has an annual learning budget of $750 for courses, books, and conferences. Managers may approve up to 3 days of paid learning time per year.

7. ON-CALL AND OVERTIME
Engineering and Support employees participate in on-call rotations, typically one week in every four. On-call staff receive a stipend of $300 per rotation week. Exempt employees are not eligible for overtime pay. Time off in lieu may be granted at the manager's discretion.

8. RECOGNITION
Peer recognition is available through the monthly "Meridian Stars" program. Spot bonuses of up to $500 may be awarded by directors.
