MERIDIAN SYSTEMS - CUSTOMER SUCCESS STRATEGY AND PLAYBOOK
Owner: VP of Customer Success. FY2026 edition.

1. MISSION
Ensure every customer achieves measurable value quickly, renews with confidence, and expands over time.

2. KEY METRICS (FY2025)
- Annual revenue churn: 11.2% (target: 8%).
- Net revenue retention: 102% (industry median 108%).
- Customer satisfaction: 4.3 out of 5.
- Average time to first value: 47 days (target: 30 days).

3. ONBOARDING PROGRAM
Every new customer receives a named onboarding manager and a milestone-based plan covering kickoff, integration, training, and go-live. Customers above $50,000 annual contract value receive a dedicated technical resource. Onboarding delays are the single largest source of early churn and the top complaint category.

4. CUSTOMER HEALTH SCORING
Each account has a health score based on product usage, support ticket trends, executive engagement, and survey responses. Accounts scoring red trigger an action plan within 5 business days and a review by the Customer Success Director.

5. ENGAGEMENT CADENCE
- Enterprise accounts: quarterly business reviews (QBRs) with executive sponsors.
- Mid-market accounts: semi-annual reviews and monthly check-ins.
- Small accounts: digital programs and webinars.

6. RENEWAL MANAGEMENT
Renewal conversations begin 120 days before contract end. Customer Success owns renewal forecasting, with Sales supporting commercial terms. Renewals at risk are escalated to the VP of Customer Success.

7. SALES-TO-SUCCESS HANDOFF
A structured handoff document is required for every new deal, listing the customer's goals, promised capabilities, and stakeholders. Audits show handoff documents were incomplete for 35% of deals in FY2025, leading to mismatched expectations.

8. FY2026 INITIATIVES
- Reduce onboarding time to 30 days through templates and automation.
- Introduce a retention component into sales compensation.
- Require Customer Success review of discounts above 20% and of non-standard commitments.
- Expand support capacity in EMEA to protect renewals.
